Training settings

Sequence challenges combine quick-time events, buttons, and stick gestures. Difficulty adapts to your recent performance. Controller button mapping can vary slightly by browser and iPadOS version. This app reads standard browser gamepad input; it does not send commands to the game itself.

Made for practice, not a test. You can pause, repeat a prompt, or switch to an easier mode at any time.
Controller detection works only when iPadOS and Safari expose the connected gamepad to the page.
